Profiles and website data

Each Lite profile has its own sign-ins and cookies. Add another profile to use a different account on the same website. Groups help organize profiles without combining their data.

If a website has trouble loading, open its App Settings and try disabling ad and tracker blocking. Some websites restrict sign-ins in embedded browsers. Clear Cache keeps sign-ins; Clear All Website Data usually signs you out of that profile.

Lite Pro and purchases

Free Lite includes 3 user-added profiles, the included starter apps, and 1 user-created group. Lite Pro adds unlimited profile and group creation, plus more profile symbols and badge colors. Privacy controls, ad blocking, and biometric locks remain available on the free plan.

Lite Pro is offered as a weekly or monthly auto-renewing subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Eligible new monthly subscribers receive a 1-week free trial. Weekly and lifetime plans do not include a free trial. Prices and billing periods are shown in your local currency before purchase. Subscriptions renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

In Lite, open Settings → Lite Pro → Restore Purchases to restore a purchase made with your Apple Account. Use Manage Subscriptions to review or cancel a subscription. Deleting Lite does not cancel a subscription.

If Pro ends, your saved profiles, groups, bookmarks, downloads, and saved customization stay accessible. Free limits apply to new profiles and groups. Lifetime access does not automatically cancel a previously purchased subscription; manage any existing subscription through Apple.

Purchases are subject to Apple’s Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

Privacy policy

Effective September 22, 2026

Information stored on your device

Lite stores your profile library, groups, bookmarks, preferences, and downloaded files locally. WebKit maintains isolated website data for each profile. Websites you visit receive network requests and information you provide to them, under their own privacy policies. Lite does not transmit your browsing history, website cookies, sign-in credentials, bookmarks, or downloads to the developer or RevenueCat.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat validates purchases, maintains Lite Pro entitlements, and provides purchase analytics using purchase history, an anonymous app user ID, and technical information such as app version, locale, and currency. Lite does not provide RevenueCat with your name or email, and automatic device identifier collection is disabled. Purchase information is not used for cross-app advertising tracking.

Read RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy for their processing and retention practices.

Website icons and blocking

Lite includes catalog icons and may request icons directly from websites and their image hosts, without your sign-in cookies. Those servers receive normal network information, including your IP address. Lite includes no advertising SDK, does not sell your browsing data, and uses a bundled list to block known advertising and tracking resources when blocking is enabled. Blocking is not a guarantee against all tracking.

Permissions and optional proxies

Camera, microphone, and biometric access depend on your permission and device settings. Lite does not receive your biometric template. If you configure your own encrypted proxy, the proxy provider processes routed traffic according to its policies. Lite does not operate a proxy or VPN service.

Your choices and retention

You can clear a profile’s website data or delete the profile in Lite. Deleting Lite removes its local library; device backups may retain copies under your backup settings. Downloaded files exported outside Lite remain where you saved them. Apple and RevenueCat may retain purchase records as needed to provide access, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations.

Support and privacy requests

To request deletion of support correspondence or ask about purchase data, contact aniketprasad123@gmail.com. We may need information identifying the relevant purchase, without asking for your Apple password.
